## Atomic summary

The Project NOVA video describes a desktop companion with a 5.5-inch OLED, an Orange Pi computer, a reflective-screen enclosure, controlled lighting, 3D-printed parts, custom PCBs, a USB camera, and a motorized display base with potentiometer position feedback. Its transcript describes face/hand tracking, object recognition, voice interaction, an Android control app, speaker/music, smart-light and AC commands, and a physical switch that cuts camera and microphone power.

The display is presented as a reflection illusion, not a free-floating volumetric hologram. The video demonstrates behavior but does not independently establish component performance, offline operation, privacy retention, actuator safety, or a reusable bill of materials.

## When it applies

Use this note when evaluating voice/avatar interfaces or camera-assisted home automation. A visual demo does not establish reliable person identification, safety, or production readiness.

## Evidence and limits

The YouTube page, public description, and transcript panel were opened on 2026-11-17. Playback was auto-dubbed to Portuguese; the transcript and part names may contain transcription errors. Hardware and software details remain creator-reported; no source files, schematics, PCB layouts, offline tests, privacy tests, or actuator-safety tests were independently reviewed. The exact Orange Pi model was not established. Nivus does not currently implement this multimodal assistant.
