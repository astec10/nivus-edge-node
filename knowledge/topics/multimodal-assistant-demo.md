## Atomic summary

A video description presents a desktop assistant that combines voice, camera-based interaction and smart-home controls. Before adapting such a demo to Nivus, validate offline behavior, device/API permissions, retention of camera and audio, physical privacy controls, and safe confirmation for actuators.

## When it applies

Use this note when evaluating voice/avatar interfaces or camera-assisted home automation. A visual demo does not establish reliable person identification, safety, or production readiness.

## Evidence and limits

The summary is based on indexed description metadata, not an independent inspection of source files or a safety test. Nivus does not currently implement this multimodal assistant.
